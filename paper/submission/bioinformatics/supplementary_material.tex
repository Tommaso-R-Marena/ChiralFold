\section{Supplementary Methods}

\subsection{Ramachandran benchmark protocol}
Era-representative stratified sampling across X-ray ultra ($\leq$1.20~\AA), high, medium, low, NMR, and cryo-EM bins (seed 20260612). mmCIF$\to$PDB conversion uses chain-ID remapping (A--Z, 0--9). Structures with $>$36 chains skipped. Script: \texttt{benchmarks/expand\_ramachandran\_benchmark.py}.

\subsection{Experimental validation protocol}
Automated cross-check of all 16 error structures: \texttt{benchmarks/experimental\_structure\_validation.py} queries RCSB entry metadata (method, resolution) and wwPDB CCD InChI stereochemistry; results in \texttt{results/experimental\_validation\_report.json}. Reproduce in Colab: \texttt{demos/D\_Residue\_Experimental\_Validation.ipynb}. PDB 5M2K exclusion documented in \texttt{results/5m2k\_benchmark\_exclusion.json} (vancomycin glycopeptide, not a globular protein).

\subsection{Mirror-image, AF3 correction, and enumeration}
L$\leftrightarrow$D reflection: $(x,y,z)\to(-x,y,z)$. Because reflection is an isometry, all pairwise distances (and therefore MolProbity-style clashscore) are unchanged before vs.\ after mirroring; this is asserted in \texttt{tests/test\_clash\_preservation.py}. AF3 correction reflects only the violating C$\alpha$ (and HA if present) across the N--C--C$\beta$ plane, preserving CA--N, CA--C, and CA--C$\beta$ bond lengths exactly and driving residual chirality violations to 0\%. Diastereomer enumeration scores $2^k$ L/D patterns. Interface scoring uses BSA, H-bonds, and salt bridges.

\subsection{Unusual cases: macrocycles, non-standard ligands, and density}
The signed-volume C$\alpha$ classifier does not require standard protein geometry. Representative edge cases (demo: \texttt{demos/Unusual\_Cases\_and\_Clash\_Safety.ipynb}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cyclic / strained peptides:} PDB 1XT7 (daptomycin) and 2RMI (astressin) --- Stereochem-class D-label/L-coordinate mismatches detected despite macrocyclic constraints.
  \item \textbf{Non-standard CCD ligands:} PDB 1OF6 deposits eight DTY codes whose coordinates and CCD InChI match L-Tyr (CCD-Code class); similarly 1BG0/1P52 (DAR$\leftarrow$L-Arg).
  \item \textbf{High-resolution density:} PDB 1HHZ (0.99~\AA) carries a DAL error, showing mismatches are not explained by low-resolution electron density alone.
  \item \textbf{Non-protein Rama exclusion:} PDB 5M2K (vancomycin--Zn$^{2+}$ glycopeptide) is excluded from the protein Ramachandran benchmark under a pre-specified outlier rule (\texttt{results/5m2k\_benchmark\_exclusion.json}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Five-minute offline reproduction}
Reviewers can recompute the 12{,}573-residue / 29-error survey without network access after clone: \texttt{python benchmarks/reproduce\_d\_residue\_errors.py} (numpy only; Colab: \texttt{demos/Reviewer\_5min\_Reproduce.ipynb}).

\subsection{AF3-mimetic correction resource benchmark}
Because AF3 outputs are not redistributed or rerun, we benchmarked synthetic AF3-mimetic Childs-system peptide PDBs with the published 51\% AF3 D-peptide chirality violation rate as the external reference. Across DP19, DP9, and DP12 (39 auditable residues; 20 synthetic inverted stereocenters), ChiralFold detected 100\% of inverted stereocenters, corrected 100\% to the expected signed-volume class, and left 0\% residual violations. The full \texttt{correct\_af3\_output} pipeline took 0.0367~s total across the three systems (mean 0.0122~s/system) with peak traced allocations below 0.14~MB; process RSS was dominated by the already-loaded Python/RDKit runtime. Data: \texttt{results/af3\_resource\_benchmark.json}.

\subsection{Lean~4 formalization}
Proofs in \texttt{formal/chirality\_nogo/} (Lean 4.28.0, Mathlib). Build: \texttt{lake build}.
