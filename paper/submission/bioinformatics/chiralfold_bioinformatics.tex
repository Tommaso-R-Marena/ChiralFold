\section{Introduction}

D-amino acids resist proteolysis and underpin mirror-image therapeutics, NRPS natural products, and bacterial cell-wall chemistry. AlphaFold~3 (AF3) produces a 51\% per-residue chirality violation rate on D-peptides \citep{childs2025}, and MolProbity---the standard PDB validator \citep{chen2010}---does not extend C$\alpha$ chirality checks to D-labeled residues. Deposited D-amino acid coordinates may therefore carry annotation errors that persist undetected.

We present ChiralFold, a pip-installable stereochemistry toolkit, and the first systematic PDB-wide audit of D-residue chirality. We classify error modes, benchmark the auditor against wwPDB/MolProbity, formalize a no-go theorem for distance-based chirality in Lean~4, and release reproducible code (v3.4.0).

\section{Materials and methods}

\subsection{Signed tetrahedron volume}

Chirality at C$\alpha$ uses the signed volume of the tetrahedron formed by backbone atoms N, C$\alpha$, C, and C$\beta$:
\begin{equation}
V = (\mathbf{N}-\mathbf{C}_\alpha)\cdot\left[(\mathbf{C}-\mathbf{C}_\alpha)\times(\mathbf{C}_\beta-\mathbf{C}_\alpha)\right].
\end{equation}
For correctly deposited D-residues, $V<0$ by CIP convention; $V>0$ at a D-label indicates L-coordinates. The PDB survey used an independent numpy-only script (no ChiralFold imports). mmCIF-only entries ($n=245$) were excluded from legacy-PDB coverage statistics.

\subsection{ChiralFold auditor}

The auditor scores C$\alpha$ chirality, bond geometry, hybrid Ramachandran grids, peptide planarity, steric clashes, and a composite 0--100 score. Ramachandran calibration used an era-representative stratified sample of 362 standard-protein structures (seed 20260612), comparing outlier percentages to wwPDB validation XML; one non-protein entry (5M2K) was excluded under a pre-specified criterion (\texttt{wwpdb\_rama\_outlier\_pct}$>50\%$). See Supplementary Methods for bin definitions and chain-ID remapping.

\subsection{Formal verification}

Lean~4 proofs (\texttt{formal/chirality\_nogo/}) show that reflection preserves pairwise distances but negates oriented volume, and that any model factoring through the distance matrix cannot represent signed chirality. AF3 correction reflects violating C$\alpha$ atoms across the N--C--C$\beta$ plane; mirror-image and diastereomer modules are described in Supplementary Methods.

\section{Results}

\subsection{PDB D-residue survey}

Of 12{,}573 checkable D-residues, 29 (0.23\%) in 16 structures showed positive signed volume despite D-labels (Fig.~\ref{fig:ccd}). Cross-referencing deposition metadata and literature yields six genuine stereochemistry errors (biology requires D, coordinates show L), 18 CCD-code misassignments across five structures (L-ligand labeled with D-code), three polymer mislabels, and two borderline cases (Supplementary Table~S1). All 29 mismatches map to nine CCD codes; nine codes are confirmed clean at zero errors (Fisher's exact $p=1.96\times10^{-10}$); DTY and DLY alone account for 19 errors. MolProbity's standard C$\alpha$ chirality check applies to L-residues only and does not flag these D-label mismatches. Thirteen of 16 error structures predate 2006 wwPDB remediation (Mann--Whitney $p=1.14\times10^{-4}$ on the expanded set); resolution does not predict errors ($p=0.19$).

\subsection{MolProbity calibration}

On $n=362$ structures, ChiralFold Ramachandran outlier percentages agree with wwPDB (Spearman $\rho=0.52$, 95\% CI [0.42, 0.61], $p=1.5\times10^{-26}$; mean 0.78\% vs 0.83\%; Fig.~\ref{fig:rama}). Pearson $r$ is outlier-sensitive (0.33 for $n=363$; 0.85 after excluding the sole non-protein entry 5M2K); Spearman $\rho$ is the primary agreement metric. ChiralFold adds D-residue chirality auditing absent from MolProbity.

\subsection{Formal obstruction and AF3}

Lean~4 proofs establish that any chirality readout factoring only through pairwise distances cannot determine sign (Supplementary Table~S2); this is a structural limitation of distance-only representations, not a complete model of SE(3)-equivariant predictors. On 41 D-peptide sequences from \citet{childs2025}, ChiralFold yields 0/478 violations by explicit SMILES construction (construction-vs-learning contrast, not fold prediction). A synthetic AF3-mimetic resource benchmark further shows 100\% detection/correction recall, 0\% residual violations, and a 0.0367~s local correction pipeline across three Childs-system peptides (Supplementary Fig.~S4), supporting correction as lightweight stereochemistry post-processing rather than AF3 re-inference.

\section{Discussion}

The signed C$\alpha$ volume distinguishes stereochemistry errors, CCD misassignments, and mislabels without biological context. The 1HHZ case (0.99~\AA\ resolution, D-Ala with $V=+2.70$) shows errors are labeling failures, not density limitations. CCD misassignments are fixable by comparing deposited coordinates to the InChI \texttt{/m} flag at deposition \citep{shao2015,outcome2016}.

ChiralFold is a stereochemistry post-processor, not a de novo folder: use AF3 or ESMFold for folding, then audit or correct with ChiralFold. Limitations include mmCIF-only exclusions (245 structures), chi1-only rotamer scoring, single-investigator error subtyping from metadata and literature, two borderline cases with signed volume $<1.0$~\AA$^3$, and no experimental binding validation of mirror-designed peptides.

\section{Conclusion}

ChiralFold identifies D-residue annotation errors invisible to existing pipelines and motivates a one-line deposition check: compare signed C$\alpha$ volume to the selected CCD stereochemistry flag. Code, data, formal proofs, and reproduction scripts are publicly available (v3.4.0).
