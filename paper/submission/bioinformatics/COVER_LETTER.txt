Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled "ChiralFold: systematic detection of D-amino acid stereochemistry errors in the Protein Data Bank" for publication as an Original Paper in Bioinformatics.

D-amino acids are increasingly important in therapeutics and natural-product structural biology, yet no systematic audit of deposited D-residue stereochemistry has been reported, and MolProbity does not flag D-label/L-coordinate mismatches. We verified 12,573 D-residues across 4,616 PDB files and identified 29 annotation errors in 16 structures, classified as genuine stereochemistry errors, CCD-code misassignments, or polymer mislabels. We introduce ChiralFold, a pip-installable auditor calibrated against wwPDB on 362 era-representative structures (Ramachandran Spearman rho = 0.52), with AF3 chirality correction and machine-checked Lean 4 proofs establishing that distance-only models cannot determine chirality sign.

This work fits Bioinformatics's scope in computational structural biology and open software: code is on PyPI (chiralfold 3.4.0), Docker (GHCR), and GitHub with 48 automated tests, full reproduction scripts, and archived benchmark outputs. The PDB survey motivates a concrete wwPDB deposition recommendation testable with one cross product per residue.

We can provide a list of suggested reviewers upon request. Candidates with expertise in PDB validation (MolProbity/wwPDB), D-peptide stereochemistry, or macromolecular deposition pipelines would be appropriate. We have no conflicts of interest with editorial board members.

The manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors approve this submission.
