\paragraph{Machine-checked scope of the chirality obstruction.}
Machine-checked Lean~4 proofs establish that the six pairwise distances among
four points in $\mathbb{R}^3$ determine the squared magnitude, but not the sign,
of their oriented tetrahedral volume.  More generally, orthogonal coordinate
transformations preserve the full pairwise distance matrix, whereas an improper
orthogonal transformation (determinant $-1$) reverses oriented volume.  It
follows that any representation whose entire geometric dependence factors
through the pairwise distance matrix is invariant on an enantiomeric pair and
cannot reproduce either signed volume or its Boolean sign on all configurations.
For the atom order $(\mathrm{N},\mathrm{C}_{\alpha},\mathrm{C},\mathrm{C}_{\beta})$,
this proves that the C$\alpha$ signed-volume chirality surrogate is not a
function of those six distances alone.  The result motivates ChiralFold's use of
signed volume as an explicit, non-distance stereochemical audit.

\noindent\textbf{Limitation.}
These theorems characterize distance-only representations; they are not an
impossibility result for AlphaFold~3 or for general SE(3)-equivariant predictors,
which may incorporate angular, tensorial, higher-order, sequence-derived, or
other orientation-sensitive features.
